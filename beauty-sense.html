The Salon

A private haven in Al Mamzar

Beauty & Sense was born of a simple wish — that every woman in our neighbourhood would have somewhere she could exhale. Somewhere ladies-only, where the hands that work on her hair, her skin and her nails have done so a thousand times.

Founded and led by Rahila Naeem, our salon on 54 St has grown by word of mouth alone — through the daughters, the sisters and the brides of Al Mamzar.

Today we are home to a team of stylists, makeup artists and nail technicians from across the region, each chosen for skill and for warmth. Open daily until 11 in the evening — because every woman deserves a chair after work.
