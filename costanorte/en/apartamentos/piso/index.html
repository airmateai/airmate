Description

The apartments are just 50 metres from the beach and Oyambre Natural Park. The centre of San Vicente is a 10-minute walk away. There is free public parking nearby.

This apartment has an open-plan space with a kitchen-dining area (fully equipped with table, chairs, appliances and cookware) and a living room with TV and sofa bed (sleeps 2).

In a separate room there is a bedroom with a double bed and a sofa bed.

On the seafront promenade, next to Costa Norte school and Oyambre Natural Park. The centre of San Vicente is a ten-minute walk away and there is free public parking right next door.
