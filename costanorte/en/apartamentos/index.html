      Surf camps, teams or families: we put several apartments and the lessons together in a single booking.

      Add surfing to your stay

      When booking you choose how many lessons and for how many people. With lessons, both the accommodation and the lessons get the surf camp discount.

      Group surf lessons

      Two-hour lessons, beginner or improver, with board and wetsuit.

      Private lesson

      An instructor just for you or your group, at your own pace.

      Paddle surf

      On the estuary or the waves of Merón. From age 7.
