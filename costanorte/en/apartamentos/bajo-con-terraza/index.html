Description

Charming one-bedroom apartment with a living-dining room, just 100 metres from the beach and Oyambre Natural Park. The centre of San Vicente is a 15-minute walk away. Fully equipped kitchen with dishwasher, fridge and microwave for extra comfort. Spacious living room and a private terrace to enjoy the outdoors.

Enjoy the peace and the closeness of the sea in this cosy, carefully decorated home.

On the seafront promenade, next to Costa Norte school and Oyambre Natural Park. The centre of San Vicente is a ten-minute walk away and there is free public parking right next door.
